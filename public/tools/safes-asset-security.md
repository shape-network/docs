# Safes & Asset Security

Manage high-value assets and team operations securely on Shape.

- **Safe**: Multi-signature wallet for team treasuries, protocol governance, or any operation requiring multiple approvals. Shape's Safe is at [safe.shape.network](https://safe.shape.network/).
- **Delegate**: Prove NFT ownership or claim airdrops from a hot wallet without moving assets from cold storage.

- [Safe](https://safe.shape.network/): A decentralized custody protocol and collective asset management platform

- [Delegate](https://delegate.xyz/): Claim airdrops, mint NFTs, and prove ownership via your hot wallet
