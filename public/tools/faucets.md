# Faucets

Get free testnet ETH on Shape Sepolia for development.

1. Go to the Alchemy or Optimism faucet
2. Sign in with an account (free tier works)
3. Enter your wallet address
4. Receive testnet ETH (usually within seconds)

- [Alchemy Faucet](https://www.alchemy.com/faucets/shape-sepolia)

- [Optimism Faucet](https://console.optimism.io/faucet)

> You can also [bridge Sepolia ETH](https://docs.shape.network/tools/bridges.md) to get started.
