# Registering Your Contract for Gasback

There are two different methods to register your contract for Gasback registration. You can either use the UI, or register programmatically from the contract you wish to register. This section has tutorials on how to do each approach.

- [Using the dashboard](https://docs.shape.network/tutorials/registering-contract-gasback/dashboard.md)

- [Programmatically](https://docs.shape.network/tutorials/registering-contract-gasback/programmatically.md)
