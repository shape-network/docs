# Superchain incentives

Learn about the Superchain incentives available to you as a Shape builder.

Shape is a part of the [Superchain](https://app.optimism.io/superchain/). This means when you build on Shape, you become eligible for additional incentives, including:

- [RetroPGF](https://app.optimism.io/retropgf): Rewarding your onchain impact

- [Free contract deployment](https://docs.optimism.io/builders/app-developers/tutorials/deploy-for-free): Claim deployment rebates

- [Gas sponsorship](https://console.optimism.io/): For your early users

- [Launch support](https://console.optimism.io/): Amplified marketing through Superchain channels
